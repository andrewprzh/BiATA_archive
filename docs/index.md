# Bioinformatics: from Algorithms to Applications

**BiATA** was an international conference series held annually from 2017 to 2021
in Saint Petersburg, Russia (online in 2020 and 2021), organized at Saint Petersburg
State University by the Center for Algorithmic Biotechnology.

> Modern life science studies involve both biological and computational methods.
> Breakthrough results in these fields can only be achieved by tight communication
> and knowledge exchange between the two sides. The ultimate goal of the conference
> is to bring together bioinformaticians and biologists to share experience in
> development and practical application of bioinformatics algorithms.

This site archives the invited speakers, committees, schedules, books of abstracts and photos of each edition.
