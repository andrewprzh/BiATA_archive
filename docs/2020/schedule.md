# BiATA 2020 schedule

[:material-arrow-left: BiATA 2020](index.md)
{ .sched-back }

All times are Moscow time (UTC+3). The schedule is taken from the conference's [Sched page](https://biata2020.sched.com/), which also has the talk abstracts.

BiATA 2020 was held online. Talks and keynotes were pre-recorded and released to participants in advance; the sessions below are the live Q&A sessions and the MGnify workshop, which ran in parallel.
