## About

The fifth international conference «Bioinformatics: from Algorithms to Applications»
was held online on July 12–13, 2021, with pre-recorded talks released to participants
in advance. It was followed by a two-day online workshop on metagenomic data analysis
with MGnify (July 14–15).

**Topics:** Sequencing technologies · Molecular sequence analysis · Computational genomics ·
Genome assembly · Transcriptomics · Metagenomics · Agrigenomics · Viromics · CRISPR ·
Natural products discovery
